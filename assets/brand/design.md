# A Little Company visual direction

The creature concept was rejected by the caregiver as potentially unsettling. Do not use icon.svg or companion.svg in the app. Revised candidate: a simple rounded leaf silhouette with generous empty space. Keep the child's interaction entirely voice-driven. Specific sensory preferences are awaiting caregiver input; this design is not a claim of universal suitability.

Palette: lagoon #285E62, ink #24484C, butter #F3CF78, coral #D97560, pale sky #EAF4F2, leafy sage #B9CDB5.

Typography: Avenir Next Rounded-style system body and a friendly rounded display treatment. Generous sentence-case headings; no tracked uppercase labels.

Layout: wide iPad canvas with greeting and listening feedback on the left, a large companion illustration on the right, a low garden horizon, and a discreet caregiver control. Portrait stacks greeting above the companion. Music appears as one compact now-playing strip.

Motion: static by default. No automatic bobbing, pulsing, blinking, or mouth animation. Use concise text to communicate listening and speaking states. Any future motion must be optional and honor reduced-motion preferences.

Artwork source: generated through the user's signed-in Freepik website in Chrome, which redirects to Magnific. Keep source downloads and editable vector assets in this directory; include only runtime assets in the iOS asset catalog.
